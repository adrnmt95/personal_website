\section{Job Market Paper}
\begin{papers}
\item \textbf{When Governments Look Away: Extremist Violence in the West Bank} (single-authored)\\
\emph{Why do governments fail to restrain non-state violence when they have the capacity to stop it?} I develop a model of institutional forbearance, in which a government relaxes enforcement when private violence advances goals it partly shares, and test it in the West Bank. The 2016 U.S. election, and the December 2017 Jerusalem Declaration, lowered the external cost to Israel of tolerating radical settlers. Using a difference-in-differences design on a fine spatial grid, I compare locations with high and low pre-election suitability for outpost expansion. Settler violence against Palestinians rises sharply and persistently in high-suitability locations, concentrates where further expansion is feasible rather than where settlers already live, and is followed by new outposts in the same places. After fatal attacks, right-wing Knesset blocs demand enforcement far more readily when the perpetrator is Palestinian than when it is a settler. Data: ACLED events classified with LLMs, settlement and outpost maps, Sentinel-2 and Maxar imagery, and the Knesset corpus.
\end{papers}

% ---------- Working papers ----------
\section{Working Papers}
\begin{papers}
\item \textbf{The Audience and the Agenda: Electoral Accountability and the Public Record}, with Sergio Galletta. \emph{Under review.} \href{https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5110510}{SSRN (2025)}.\\
The Seventeenth Amendment moved U.S. senators from indirect to direct election. Senators whose inherited partisan base was weakest statewide broadened their floor-speech agendas once direct election became credible; roll-call positions did not move.
\item \textbf{Mafia Connections: Infiltration in Corporate Ownership}, with Monica Billio, Marco Di Cataldo, and Giovanni Mastrobuoni.\\
Temporal graph network embeddings of a dynamic Italian ownership network anchored on 5,700 judicially confiscated firms. The embeddings forecast confiscation up to four years ahead and date firms' transitions into a high-risk regime; around that transition, firms expand and become illiquid, consistent with use as financial conduits.
\item \textbf{The Social Multiplier of Leisure: Peer Effects in Museum Attendance}, with Pasquale Accardo and Giovanni Mastrobuoni. \href{https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/publications/the-social-multiplier-of-leisure-peer-effects-in-museum-attendanc}{University of Bath Working Paper Series}.\\
Peer effects in museum attendance and membership renewal from administrative cardholder data in Piedmont, identified with age-based price discontinuities.
\end{papers}
